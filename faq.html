FAQs

All that noise and all that sound

Are children invited?

We love the little people in our lives, but our wedding day will be an adults-only celebration - Outside of the children in our wedding party a part of the ceremony. Thank you for understanding and making arrangements in advance.

May I bring a guest?

Because of venue capacity and planning details, we are only able to accommodate the guests named on your invitation. Thank you for understanding.

Is the wedding unplugged?

Yes. We are asking guests to put phones and cameras away during the ceremony, cocktail hour, and reception so everyone can be fully present. We’ll happily share professional photos and videos afterward.

What should I wear?

Our dress code is formal attire. We invite you to dress up to help us celebrate our marriage! The celebration is at a countryside venue, so please keep grass, cobblestone, gravel, stairs, and September weather in mind when choosing shoes and layers. Long gowns or skirts, suits and ties (no tuxedos) are preferred.

Are there colours I should not wear?

Yes. We ask guests not to wear white (including patterns on mostly white bases), dark brown, dark green, or pomegranate, as these are the colours the wedding party will be wearing. Instead, please wear formal attire in bright colours and/or fun patterns. If you have any doubts or questions about your outfit ideas, please contact the bride and/or groom.

Will dietary restrictions be accommodated?

Yes. When completing your RSVP, there will be a place to share dietary restrictions and allergies so we can pass them along to the venue.

Is there an open bar?

Yes, there will be an open bar. The bar will pause service during dinner service so you can enjoy your meal uninterrupted. To keep the celebration fun and safe, all alcoholic beverages must stay within the licensed areas: Inside the venue, just outside the front door to the venue, and on the designated patio of the venue. Guests under the age of 19 will not be served alcohol.

Can I smoke on the property?

Smoking cigarettes and vapes is permitted at least 5 meters away from doors and on grassy areas away from patios.

Can I Uber or taxi from the venue?

Strathmere is just outside Ottawa, so rideshare and taxi availability may be limited late at night. Please plan your transportation in advance, especially since there will not be a shuttle. See the ‘Travel & Venue’ tab of our website for more information.

What happens if it rains?

The ceremony and cocktail hour are planned outdoors, weather permitting. If the weather does not cooperate, the venue plan will shift indoors. If the ceremony and cocktail hour are outdoors as planned, come prepared for sun.

Are there accommodations available on site?

Strathmere does offer some overnight accommodations. However, space is limited and booked on a first-come basis. Contact Strathmere for information on availability.
